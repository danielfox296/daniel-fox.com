The ceiling

The growth strategies that got you here have a ceiling.

Every tactic has its place, and referrals, repeat buyers, word of mouth and a reputation you earned all really work, to a point. Past that point, the same tactics hit diminishing returns.

And the marketing you do have (a freelancer here, an agency there, a platform you're told to post on) isn't getting the attention and expertise it needs. Time and money go in, and no one's accountable for what comes back.

The next stage comes from fresh eyes, practices that work, and putting someone qualified in charge.
