Who it fits

Established, profitable consumer businesses, typically $3 to $12M, that have outgrown referral-and-marketplace luck. Usually there's real spend already moving every month, an agency or a few freelancers doing activity, and no senior person deciding whether it's the right work. The owner is carrying the marketing decisions on top of running the company, and wants it handled by someone accountable rather than added to their plate.

One filter, because it saves us both time: this works for owners who want a leader to hand the function to and a number that leader answers for. If the search is for the cheapest possible option, the fit probably isn't there yet, and I'll say so early.
